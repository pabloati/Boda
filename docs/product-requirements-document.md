# Product Requirements Document (PRD) - Digital Wedding Invitation

## 1. Product Vision
Deliver a digital wedding invitation that matches the luxury feel of physical stationery while providing the interactivity, convenience, and responsiveness of modern web technology.

## 2. Target Audience
Wedding guests of all ages accessing the invitation primarily through mobile smartphones via messaging apps (WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS, Email).

## 3. Key User Journeys
1. **Unveiling**: Guest opens link &rarr; Sees sealed envelope with couple's monogram &rarr; Clicks "ABRIR" &rarr; Envelope opens smoothly with romantic music &rarr; Invitation card is revealed.
2. **Exploration**: Guest scrolls through the story, quotes, wedding countdown, venue locations with map links, and the day's timeline.
3. **Action (RSVP)**: Guest fills out the RSVP form (names, plus-one count, dietary restrictions, shuttle request) &rarr; Clicks "Confirmar por WhatsApp" &rarr; WhatsApp opens with pre-composed text sent directly to the couple.
4. **Logistics & Gifting**: Guest copies IBAN in 1 click, checks suggested hotels, and discovers the dress code color palette.
