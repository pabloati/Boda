# Decisions

Short log of choices made for the wedding site. One line each, newest first.

- 2026-11-19 — Bus map, four additions (`js/mapa.js`): a street, town or metro name that a stop's drawing or its name would cover is hidden (`hideCovered`, worked out again whenever the zoom changes); two quick taps or clicks on the map zoom in on that spot; a sideways drag with one finger on the page shows «Usa dos dedos para mover el mapa» for two seconds (an up-and-down drag is the page scrolling, so it shows nothing); the card of a stop links to «Cómo llegar», Google Maps directions to the stop's `dest` from wherever the guest is (the way-back card keeps its two place links).
- 2026-11-19 — Bus map: metro stations near the church and the Madrid stops (an «M» mark and the name, entries with `cls: 'metro'` in `PLACES` of `js/mapa.js`, positions looked up in OpenStreetMap's Nominatim search because the Overpass servers were down). They show from the same zoom as the main street names. The view buttons under the map are bigger (1.3rem, 1.2rem on phones, two rows there).
- 2026-11-19 — Bus map on phones: the La Cigüeña drawing sits below and to the right of its point, name underneath (its name touched «Cibeles»). Frames under 340 px get slightly smaller drawings so the far view still fits the drawn area, and the A-4 label hides in the far view on phones (it sat under Cibeles).
- 2026-11-19 — Story photos, first trial (branch `introduccion`): Madrid = the kiss in the street, Copenhague = the winter selfie, Valencia = Fallas, exported at 1100 px wide to `assets/historia-1.jpg` … `-3.jpg`. The RSVP sending card (`assets/foto-envio.jpg`) is now the Cibeles selfie, cropped square; the old one had a meme caption. The originals stay in `assets/nosotros/`, local only (11 MB, not committed).
- 2026-11-19 — Introduction as an album (branch `introduccion`): a two-sentence welcome, three prints for the three stages (Madrid, Copenhague, Valencia) and straight into the countdown; the closing line is gone. The text of each stage is on the back of its print: a tap lifts the print to the middle of the screen and turns it over (there, each tap on the print turns it again, text or photo; a tap outside, Esc or scrolling sends it back). Until a guest opens one, the prints take turns to swing round and show their back, and a handwritten line under them says «Toca una foto para darle la vuelta». On phones: two prints side by side and the third below.
- 2026-11-18 — Introduction (branch `introduccion`, layout with placeholder texts): the olive quote band (1 Juan 4:18) is replaced by «Nuestra historia» in the same `#intro` section: a welcome, three moments each with a photo shown as a paper print with a handwritten caption (`assets/historia-1.jpg` … `-3.jpg`, dashed frame until the file exists), and a closing line that runs straight into the countdown. The pieces fade in as the guest scrolls (`.reveal`, `js/script.js` 10b). The couple writes every text.
- 2026-11-18 — Scroll reveal recovered: the content of each section fades up the first time it comes into view (`js/script.js` 10b, `.js-reveal .reveal` in the CSS). It had only existed on the local branch `redesign` (2026-10-19) and never reached `main`. Rebuilt so the script picks the pieces itself (section content blocks; in the Hoja de ruta the heading and each stop) and with the `translate` property, so it does not clash with the editor's text positions. Off without JavaScript and under reduced motion. Also: on phones the «Desliza» hint now fades as the lettering lifts, instead of sliding over the faces.
- 2026-11-19 — Bus map, street names (branch `mapa-ajustes`, asked by Mª Ángeles): zooming into the church / Cibeles and Avenida de América shows the names of the main streets, drawn along each street (four per area from a middle zoom, the rest close up; by La Cigüeña, where streets have no names, the road numbers). Positions and angles come from the map data with `_dev/mapa_calles.py`. The round drawings and every name on the map are bigger and bold (Cormorant 700 added to the font link). The Iglesia and Salida views open about 1.2 km wide instead of as close as possible, so the streets around are in sight.
- 2026-11-18 — Bus map, second round (branch `mapa-ajustes`): the Victorian corners are gone and the frame is a plain line in the terracotta of the route; the round drawings are bigger (`K` in `js/mapa.js`, smaller on phone-width frames); the far view shows A-3, A-4, Rivas and Arganda del Rey; and the starting view is now searched as the closest one where every drawing and name fits whole (`viewFor`), on a taller 4:5 frame on phones.
- 2026-11-17 — Bus map (branch `mapa-buses`, prototype): a section after the Hoja de ruta with a map "like the one of a video game" that guests can zoom and drag. It is a real map of Madrid–Arganda redrawn in the site's colours (`assets/mapa.svg`, from OpenStreetMap data, credit line required), with the church, Cibeles (bus at 14:00), La Cigüeña and the return stops (Cibeles and Avenida de América, around 23:00) as round drawings; tapping one shows its card and a Google Maps link. Chosen over an embedded Google Map (slow, off-style) and over a map library (the site has no dependencies). Frame (2026-11-18): the couple's own Victorian corner ornament (`assets/marco-esquina.png`, cut from their `Marco victoriano.png`), mirrored on the four corners and joined by a single line (the thin second line of the drawing was dropped); a first try with a thick black frame and colour flowers was rejected as out of style.
- 2026-11-17 — Photos section: the hashtag is gone; the section now links to a shared Google Photos album where guests upload their own pictures (`#album-link` in `index.html`). Link only for now; a preview strip is noted in the backlog as a future improvement.
- 2026-11-17 — New hero photos and song (branch `nuevas-fotos-cancion`): Granada (olive trees, the sierra at sunset) on phones and portrait tablets, the dance photo on wide screens; the background track is now `assets/Para_quedarte_acustico.mp3`. To clear the faces, the lettering sits at the top on portrait screens (scroll hint at the bottom of the screen on phones) and at the bottom left on wide screens. The file names `hero-desktop.jpg` / `hero-mobile.jpg` are kept, so the editor's photo slots still work.
- 2026-11-17 — Site editor: sections can be added (a heading and a paragraph, `<section id="extra-N">` before the footer, texts `extraN.*`) and the added ones deleted, and any section deleted for real (the original ones too, at Pablo's request; refused on `main`), each as its own commit, with a padlock per section that blocks deleting, hiding, moving and restyling it. A zoom control (and Ctrl + wheel) draws the page up to 4× for finer placing. Fixed along the way: appending an English text to `js/i18n.js` after a last entry without a comma produced invalid JS.
- 2026-11-17 — Site editor: the phone view no longer stretches to the window's height. It shows a fixed screen (default iPhone 12 – 14, 390 × 664 visible) with a model picker above it, and scales the whole phone down when the window is too short, so one screen in the editor is one screen on that phone.
- 2026-11-17 — Site editor: the top bar is redrawn (icons from an inline SVG sprite, grouped tools, the status in the flexible middle, labels that fold on narrow windows; icons are fine here because the no-icons rule is for the guests' page) and gains «Sincronizar», which fetches from GitHub, brings in what was pushed from elsewhere and pushes the saves GitHub lacks, with a badge counting both. It never pushes `main` and undoes any merge that really conflicts.
- 2026-11-11 — Site editor (`_dev/flores.html`, local only) gains: a Fuentes tab (curated free Google Fonts per role) and a per-text style in the Flores view (size, colour, alignment, font); writing directly on the page in Textos; background (paper / dark paper / green) and spacing per section; a Historial tab whose «Volver a este punto» adds a new save instead of rewriting history; a 409 warning plus a one-minute poll when someone else saved on the branch, and per-branch drafts in the browser; «Ver en mi móvil» (preview link + locally drawn QR); and «Publicar», which merges the branch into `main` and pushes it after a dry-run list, refusing on conflicts.
